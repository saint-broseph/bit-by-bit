CONCISE BREAKDOWN
- The Goal: Send n messages at specific times m[i] starting from time 0.
- The Objective: Determine if the initial charge f is enough to send all messages.
- The Constraint: 
  1. Staying on costs 'a' per unit of time.
  2. Turning off and back on costs 'b' flat.
  3. Charge must remain > 0 at all times (f <= 0 means failure).

CORE CONCEPTS
- Greedy Strategy: For every interval between two messages, choose the cheaper option between leaving the phone on or turning it off and on.
- Simulation: Track the charge consumption step-by-step from time 0 through all message timestamps.

LOGIC AND IMPLEMENTATION
1. Initialize 'current_time' at 0.
2. For each message at time m[i]:
   - Calculate the time elapsed: delta = m[i] - current_time.
   - Calculate cost to stay on: cost_on = delta * a.
   - Calculate cost to restart: cost_off_on = b.
   - Subtract the minimum of (cost_on, cost_off_on) from the current charge f.
   - Update 'current_time' to m[i].
3. After processing a message, if f <= 0, Stepan cannot proceed.
4. If all messages are processed and f > 0, the answer is YES.
